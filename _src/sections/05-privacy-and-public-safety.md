Digital surveillance creates a balance between public safety and personal privacy. Law enforcement agencies use surveillance technology to help investigate crimes. However collecting and storing large amounts of data can be dangerous for the individuals. 

### Finding a Balance

Some important questions to consider include:

- How can people know when their information is being collected?
- Who should be responsible for protecting stored information?
- What rules should exist to prevent misuse?

### Why Accountability Matters

Surveillance technology should have clear rules about how information is collected, stored, and shared. People should also have ways to understand how these systems are being used.

The [American Civil Liberties Union (ACLU)](https://www.aclu.org/issues/privacy-technology/surveillance-technologies) is worth exploring because it discusses surveillance technologies, civil liberties, and the importance of protecting people's privacy.
